# Payroll overview

Give a clear snapshot of payroll status.

## Workflow

1. Gather upcoming deadlines, recent payroll runs, and tax liability from Gusto tools.
2. Present amounts, pay periods, and due dates in plain language.
3. **Verify numbers with the user** before they act on deadlines or liabilities.
4. Offer next steps (detail a run, list employees on a payroll, check contractors) without running payroll unless asked and confirmed.

## Rules

- Treat all figures as sensitive.
- Do not run or submit payroll from this skill without following **gusto-write-safely**.
