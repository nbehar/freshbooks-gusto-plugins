# People lookup

Find people and key HR dates in Gusto.

## Workflow

1. Clarify whether they need employees, contractors, managers, or everyone.
2. Query Gusto for the matching people and fields (role, manager, birthday, start date, status).
3. Present a concise list; avoid dumping unnecessary PII.
4. If the request is ambiguous, confirm identity (name vs email vs ID) before deeper lookups.

## Rules

- Minimize sensitive personal data in summaries.
- Do not change employment or onboarding data without **gusto-write-safely** confirmation.
